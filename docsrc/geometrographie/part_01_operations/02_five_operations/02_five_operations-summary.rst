The Five Elementary Operations & Coefficients S and E — Summary
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Executive Summary & Complexity Metrics (Exposition, pp. 3–5, fols. ``0019–0021``)

.. folio:: 0019
   :page: pp. 3–5

* **Scope**: *Exposition* of the five canonical operations and algebraic invariants (fols. ``0019``–``0021``, pp. 3–5).
* **Five Canonical Operations**: :math:`R_1` (ruler point-alignment), :math:`R_2` (draw line), :math:`C_1` (compass leg on determined point), :math:`C_2` (compass leg on indeterminate point of traced locus), :math:`C_3` (draw circle/arc).
* **Four Exact Invariants**:
  * Simplicity :math:`S = l_1 + l_2 + m_1 + m_2 + m_3`
  * Exactitude :math:`E = l_1 + m_1 + m_2` (lower :math:`E` = higher graphical precision)
  * Lines traced :math:`N_{\text{lines}} = l_2`, Circles traced :math:`N_{\text{circles}} = m_3`
* **Circle Shorthand**: :math:`O(AB)` denotes the circle centered at :math:`O` with radius :math:`AB`; :math:`O(R)` denotes center :math:`O`, radius :math:`R`.
