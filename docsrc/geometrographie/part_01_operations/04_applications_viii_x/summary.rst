Applications VIII–X: Segment Transfer & Equidistant Points — Summary
====================================================================

.. rubric:: Executive Summary & Complexity Metrics (Apps. VIII–X, p. 6, fols. ``0022``)

.. folio:: 0022
   :page: p. 6

* **Scope**: Applications VIII, IX, and X (fol. ``0022``, p. 6).
* **Application VIII**: Segment transfer on a traced line in :math:`2C_1 + C_2 + C_3` (indeterminate origin) or :math:`3C_1 + C_3` (fixed origin), :math:`S = 4, E = 3`, saving 12 operations relative to Euclid I.2–I.3 (:math:`S = 16`, :ref:`concordance-transfer-segment`).
* **Application IX**: Arbitrary circle through two points :math:`A, B` without tracing the perpendicular bisector: :math:`3C_1 + 3C_3` (:math:`S = 6, E = 3`, 0 lines, 3 circles).
* **Application X**: Equidistant point :math:`C` from :math:`A, B`: :math:`2C_1 + 2C_3` (:math:`S = 4, E = 2`, 0 lines, 2 circles).
