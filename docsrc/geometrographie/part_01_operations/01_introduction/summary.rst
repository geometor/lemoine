Introduction: Aim & Ideal Postulates of Geometrography — Summary
================================================================

.. rubric:: Executive Summary & Complexity Metrics (Intro., pp. 1–2, fols. ``0017–0018``)

.. folio:: 0017
   :page: pp. 1–2

* **Scope**: Opening *Introduction* of Lemoine's Pau memoir (*La Géométrographie*, fols. ``0017``–``0018``, pp. 1–2).
* **Core Thesis**: Distinguishes **speculative simplicity** (verbal and deductive brevity in textbook proofs since :person:`Euclid <euclid>`) from **geometrographic simplicity** (minimal physical operations of straightedge and compass).
* **Idealized Postulates**:
  1. Unbounded drawing sheet (general plane, no margin clipping).
  2. Single ungraduated straightedge of arbitrary length and modern divider compass retaining its aperture when lifted or while the straightedge is used.
  3. Discrete alignment accounting (:math:`E`) separated from physical drafting error analysis (:person:`d'Ocagne <docagne>`).
  4. Canonical constructions assume data placed apart unless specified on an existing figure.
