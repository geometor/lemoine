Introduction: Aim & Ideal Postulates of Geometrography — Scholarly Notes
========================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Intro., fols. ``0017–0018``)

.. folio:: 0017
   :page: pp. 1–2

* **Primary Witness**: Émile Lemoine, *La Géométrographie ou l'art des constructions géométriques*, AFAS Congrès de Pau (1892), tirage à part Paris: Gauthier-Villars (1893), folios ``0017``–``0018`` (printed pp. 1–2).
* **Historical Lineage**: Lemoine explicitly recalls his first public presentation at the Oran AFAS Congress (30 March 1888, :cite:`lemoine-1888`) and explains why he re-evaluates all elementary constructions afresh in the 1892 Pau memoir—realizing only after 1888 that nearly every classical construction taught since :person:`Euclid <euclid>` could be simplified, sometimes by half.
* **Philosophical Connection**: Contrasts directly with :person:`Proclus <proclus>`'s commentary on *Elements* I.1–I.3 (:cite:`proclus-in-euclidem`) and P. H. J. Hoenen's epistemology of constructive *phantasia* (:cite:`hoenen-1954`): Lemoine treats the instrument-hand interface itself as an exact formal calculus.
* **Cross-References**: See :doc:`../02_five_operations/index` for the five elementary operations and :doc:`../../part_04_triangle_and_reflections/24_observations_diverses/index` (pp. 52–60) for Lemoine's extended methodological replies to :person:`Mannheim <mannheim>` and :person:`Brocard <brocard>`.
