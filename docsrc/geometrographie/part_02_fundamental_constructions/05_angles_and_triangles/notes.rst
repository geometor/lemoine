Problems XI–XVI: Copying Angles & Constructing Triangles — Scholarly Notes
==========================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Probs. XI–XVI, fols. ``0023–0025``)

.. folio:: 0023
   :page: pp. 7–9

* **Primary Witness**: Folios ``0023``–``0025`` (printed pp. 7–9).
* **Critical Errata Observed on 300-DPI Scans**:
  * On folio ``0023`` (p. 7, Problem XII), the individual step costs sum to :math:`2R_1 + 2R_2 + 8C_1 + C_2 + 5C_3` (:math:`S = 18, E = 11`), whereas the printed summary line reads :math:`6C_3` with :math:`S = 18, E = 10` (counting :math:`C_2` outside :math:`E` or misprinting :math:`5C_3` as :math:`6C_3`).
  * On folio ``0025`` (p. 9, Problem XVI), the printed text gives :math:`4R_1 + 3R_2 + 8C_1 + C_2 + 3C_3` with *simplicité 19; exactitude 12* (the strict algebraic exactitude :math:`l_1 + m_1 + m_2 = 4 + 8 + 1 = 13`, as verified in :ref:`concordance-triangle-sss`).
* **Textbook Benchmark**: Footnote (**) on folio ``0023`` (p. 7) identifies **Rouché et de Comberousse**, *Traité de Géométrie* (6th edition), as Lemoine's canonical reference for standard nineteenth-century French school constructions.
