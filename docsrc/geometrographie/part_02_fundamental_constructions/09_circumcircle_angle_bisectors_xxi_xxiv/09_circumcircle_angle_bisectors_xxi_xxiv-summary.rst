Problems XXI–XXIV: Circumcircle & Angle Bisectors — Summary
===========================================================

.. rubric:: Executive Summary & Complexity Metrics (Probs. XXI–XXIV, pp. 12–14, fols. ``0028–0030``)

.. folio:: 0028
   :page: pp. 12–14

* **Scope**: Problems XXI–XXIV (fols. ``0028``–``0030``, pp. 12–14).
* **Problem XXI** (Circumcircle, Euclid IV.5 / :math:`X(3)`): Reusing :math:`B(R)` across both mediatrices lowers cost from :math:`S = 17` to :math:`S = 15, E = 9` (4 circles, :ref:`concordance-circumcircle`).
* **Problem XXII (Angle Bisector, Euclid I.9)**: Single shared aperture reduces cost from :math:`S = 10` to :math:`S = 9, E = 5` (:ref:`concordance-angle-bisector`).
* **Problem XXIII (Arc Bisection)**: :math:`S = 7, E = 4`.
* **Problem XXIV (Inaccessible-Vertex Angle Bisector)**: Equal-radius rhombus pairing locates :math:`M` and traces the bisector in :math:`S = 23, E = 13` (3 lines, 7 circles).
