Problems XVIII & XVIII bis: Perpendicular Bisector & Point Reflection — Scholarly Notes
=======================================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Prob. XVIII, fols. ``0026``)

.. folio:: 0026
   :page: p. 10

* **Primary Witness**: Folio ``0026`` (printed p. 10).
* **Crucial Primitive**: Lemoine reuses Problem XVIII *bis*'s zero-line reflection inside Problem XX (perpendicular from an external point), Problem LVIII (:math:`S/R` and :math:`2S/R`), and his :math:`S = 27` symmedian construction (Problem LI).
* **Mascheroni Comparison**: Bisecting an *un-drawn* segment :math:`AB` by compass alone requires :math:`10C_1 + 7C_3` (:math:`S = 17`, :ref:`concordance-mascheroni-midpoint-line-intersection`), whereas reflection across a line needs only 2 circles (:math:`S = 6`) in both Lemoine and Mascheroni!
