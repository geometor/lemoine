Problems XVIII & XVIII bis: Perpendicular Bisector & Point Reflection
=====================================================================

.. rubric:: Émile Lemoine — *La Géométrographie* (English Translation, p. 10, fols. ``0026``)

.. folio:: 0026
   :page: p. 10

**XVIII.** — *To trace the perpendicular at its midpoint to a straight line bounded by two points, or to place the midpoint of a given length.*

Symbol: :math:`\mathrm{op.} : (2R_1 + R_2 + 2C_1 + 2C_3)`; simplicity 7; exactitude 4; 1 straight line, 2 circles.

.. picture:: /_static/figures/fig_perpendicular_bisector.svg
   :alt: Lemoine's S = 7 perpendicular bisector using arbitrary shared radius R > AB/2
   :caption: Problem XVIII: Arbitrary-radius perpendicular bisector PQ of AB (2R₁ + R₂ + 2C₁ + 2C₃, S = 7, E = 4 vs. Euclid's S = 8).

**XVIII bis.** — *To place the symmetric point* :g:`A'` *of a point* :g:`A` *with respect to a given straight line* :g:`BC`.

From two arbitrary points :g:`B`, :g:`C` of :g:`BC`, I describe the circles :g:`B(BA)`, :g:`C(CA)` which intersect at :g:`A'`:

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (2C_1 + 2C_2 + 2C_3)`; simplicity 6; exactitude 4; 2 circles.

One may also describe :g:`A(R)` which cuts :g:`BC` at :g:`B` and at :g:`C`; describe :g:`B(AB)`, :g:`C(AB)` which intersect at :g:`A'`:

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (3C_1 + 3C_3)`; simplicity 6; exactitude 3; 3 circles.

If the undrawn straight line :g:`BC` were given by two points :g:`B` and :g:`C`, the symbol would be:

.. math::

   \mathrm{Op.} : (4C_1 + 2C_3).
