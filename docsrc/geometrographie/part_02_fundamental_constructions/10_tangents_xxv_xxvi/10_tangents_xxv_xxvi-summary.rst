Problems XXV–XXVI: Tangents to a Given Circle — Summary
=======================================================

.. rubric:: Executive Summary & Complexity Metrics (Probs. XXV–XXVI, p. 14, fols. ``0030``)

.. folio:: 0030
   :page: p. 14

* **Scope**: Problems XXV and XXVI — Circle tangents (fol. ``0030``, p. 14).
* **Problem XXV (Tangent at Boundary Point)**: Single-line equal-radius construction :math:`2R_1 + R_2 + 4C_1 + 3C_3` (:math:`S = 10, E = 6`, 1 line, 3 circles vs. classical :math:`S = 11`, 3 lines, 1 circle).
* **Problem XXVI (External Tangents, Euclid III.17)**: Two :math:`S = 18` syntheses (:math:`5R_1 + 3R_2 + 7C_1 + 3C_3`, :math:`E = 12`; and :math:`5R_1 + 3R_2 + 6C_1 + 4C_3`, :math:`E = 11`, :ref:`concordance-external-tangents`).
