* **Primary Witness**: Folios ``0040``–``0043`` (printed pp. 24–27); **FIG. 6** on fol. ``0042`` (p. 26) and **FIG. 7** on fol. ``0043`` (p. 27).
* **Credit Attribution**: Lemoine explicitly cites *Edm.-Aug. Gouzy, de Lausanne* (*Nouvelles Annales de Mathématiques*, 1857, p. 125) on folio ``0041`` (p. 25), emphasizing that Gouzy stated the geometric construction without realizing its extraordinary operational simplicity until *Géométrographie* quantified it at :math:`S = 14`.
* **Exact Proof of FIG. 6 & FIG. 7**: Both constructions were algebraically verified in SymPy inside ``scripts/build_archival_plates.py``: in **FIG. 6**, :math:`AB^2 = 2R \cdot d(B, KA) = BC \cdot BK = MN`; in **FIG. 7**, by power of :math:`D` in :math:`O(OA)` and symmetry across diameter :math:`AO`, :math:`AM^2 = AB \cdot AD = MN`.
