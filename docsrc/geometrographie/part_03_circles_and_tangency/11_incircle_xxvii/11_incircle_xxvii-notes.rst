Problem XXVII: Incircle & Excircles of a Triangle — Scholarly Notes
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Prob. XXVII, fols. ``0031–0032``)

.. folio:: 0031
   :page: pp. 15–16

* **Primary Witness**: Folios ``0031``–``0032`` (printed pp. 15–16); **FIG. 1** appears on folio ``0031`` (p. 15).
* **Scan-Verified Geometric Fidelity**: Inspecting ``0031.png`` directly clarifies that Lemoine's actual :math:`S = 23` incircle construction does *not* draw the angle bisectors :math:`BO, CO` at all; instead it leverages :math:`AD = b, BE = a` along ray :math:`BA` so :math:`DE = 2(s - a)`, placing both contact points :math:`Q, R` and the incenter :math:`O` via two perpendicular bisectors :math:`OR, OQ` sharing the single circle :math:`A(DE)`.
* **Cross-References**: :person:`Euclid <euclid>` *Elements* IV.4 (`G.116`), :person:`Clark Kimberling <kimberling>`'s *ETC* :math:`X(1)` (:cite:`kimberling-etc`), :person:`Mascheroni <mascheroni>` Libro X § 149, and :person:`Steiner <steiner>` § 18 Third & Fifth Problems.
