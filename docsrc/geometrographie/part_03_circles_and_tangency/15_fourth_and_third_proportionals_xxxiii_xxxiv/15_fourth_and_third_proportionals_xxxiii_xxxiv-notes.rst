Problems XXXIII–XXXIV bis: Fourth & Third Proportionals — Scholarly Notes
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Probs. XXXIII–XXXIV, fols. ``0037–0040``)

.. folio:: 0037
   :page: pp. 21–24

* **Primary Witness**: Folios ``0037``–``0040`` (printed pp. 21–24); **FIG. 4** on fol. ``0037`` (p. 21) and **FIG. 5** on fol. ``0038`` (p. 22).
* **Mascheroni & Fazzari Cross-Reference**: :person:`Gaetano Fazzari <fazzari>`'s 1901 Palermo preface (:cite:`fazzari-1901`, p. xi) famously highlights :person:`Mascheroni <mascheroni>`'s concentric-circle fourth proportional (§ 93) as a classic triumph of compass-only *Géométrographie* (:math:`S = 14` within its domain).
* **Theorem Footnote (p. 23)**: Footnote (*) on folio ``0039`` notes that XXXIV(c) yields a neat metric theorem: in any triangle :math:`ARC`, the perpendicular from :math:`A` to circumradius :math:`OR` meets :math:`CR` at :math:`H` with :math:`AR^2 = RH \cdot RC`.
