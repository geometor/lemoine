* **Scope**: Problems XXXIII, XXXIV, and XXXIV *bis* (fols. ``0037``–``0040``, pp. 21–24, **FIGS. 4 & 5**).
* **Fourth Proportional (XXXIII)**: Reduces classical :math:`S = 25` to :math:`S = 22, E = 14` (power of a point, **FIG. 4**, :ref:`concordance-fourth-proportional`) and evaluates chord (**FIG. 5**) and Mascheronian concentric-circle variants.
