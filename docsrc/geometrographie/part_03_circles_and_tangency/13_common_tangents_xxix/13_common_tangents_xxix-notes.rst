Problem XXIX: Common Tangents to Two Circles — Scholarly Notes
==============================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Prob. XXIX, fols. ``0032–0035``)

.. folio:: 0032
   :page: pp. 16–19

* **Primary Witness**: Folios ``0032``–``0035`` (printed pp. 16–19); **FIG. 3** appears on folio ``0034`` (p. 18).
* **Steiner Connection**: The centers of similitude (*Aehnlichkeitspunkte*, :term:`aehnlichkeitspunkte`) constructed here in **FIG. 3** form the exact projective engine of :person:`Jakob Steiner <steiner>`'s *Zweites Kapitel* (§§ 12–13) and Sixth–Eighth Problems (:cite:`steiner-1833`), where Steiner projects an un-drawn circle onto his fixed circle :math:`M`.
* **First Columnar Accounting Table**: Folio ``0033`` (p. 17) introduces Lemoine's signature five-column ledger (:math:`R_1 | R_2 | C_1 | C_2 | C_3`) for auditing complex multi-step constructions.
