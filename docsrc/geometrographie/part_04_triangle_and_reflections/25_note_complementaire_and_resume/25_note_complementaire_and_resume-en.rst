Complementary Note & Analytical Summary (d'Ocagne, Laisant & Complete Index)
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Émile Lemoine — *La Géométrographie* (English Translation, pp. 60–66, fols. ``0076–0082``)

.. folio:: 0076
   :page: p. 60

In the concluding *Note complémentaire* (pp. 60–64), :person:`Lemoine <lemoine>` engages in a cordial scientific exchange with :person:`Maurice d'Ocagne <docagne>`, :person:`Charles-Ange Laisant <laisant>`, **Dunesme**, and **Choisy** regarding physical drafting precision, weighted operation costs, and the future expansion of *Géométrographie*:

.. folio:: 0077
   :page: p. 61

**1. Discussion with :person:`Maurice d'Ocagne <docagne>` on Exactitude vs. Physical Error (pp. 60–62).**
:person:`d'Ocagne <docagne>`—creator of nomography—raised the natural engineer's question: should elementary acts :math:`R_1, C_1, C_2` receive unequal empirical weights reflecting physical error variances, and how does :math:`E` interact with oblique intersection angles?

.. folio:: 0078
   :page: p. 62

Lemoine replies with remarkable clarity: weighting operations differently would depend on the craftsman's hand, paper grain, pencil sharpness, and instrument brand, destroying the *mathematical universality* of the symbol! By retaining unweighted integer coefficients :math:`(l_1, l_2, m_1, m_2, m_3)`, the symbol remains an exact combinatorial invariant while preserving full detail so any practitioner can apply custom empirical weights to :math:`l_1, l_2, m_1, m_2, m_3` whenever desired.

.. folio:: 0079
   :page: p. 63

**2. Correspondence with :person:`Charles-Ange Laisant <laisant>`, Dunesme & Choisy (pp. 63–64).**

.. folio:: 0080
   :page: p. 64

Lemoine acknowledges simplifications and critical suggestions communicated by :person:`Laisant <laisant>` (his future co-founder of *L'Intermédiaire des Mathématiciens* in 1894 and co-editor of the *Scientia* series), alongside engineers Dunesme and Choisy, closing the body of the Pau memoir with an invitation for geometers everywhere to benchmark further constructions—an invitation fulfilled in the 1902 *Scientia* No. 18 monograph (:cite:`lemoine-1902`) integrating :term:`Géométrographie de l'équerre <equerre>`, :term:`Géométrographie Mascheronienne <geometrographie-mascheronienne>`, and :term:`Géométrographie Steinerienne <geometrographie-steinerienne>`.

.. folio:: 0081
   :page: p. 65

*Résumé analytique par ordre de matières* (Analytical Index, pp. 65–66)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Pages 65–66 of the 1893 Gauthier-Villars print present Lemoine's canonical *Résumé analytique*, indexing the entire treatise in exact structural order:

* **Introduction** (pp. 1–2) & **Exposition** (pp. 3–5): :doc:`Part I <../../part_01_operations/index>`
* **Applications I–X** (pp. 5–6): Elementary straightedge and compass primitives — :doc:`Part I <../../part_01_operations/index>`
* **Applications XI–XXVI** (pp. 7–14): Angles, triangles, parallels, bisectors, reflection, perpendiculars, circumcircle, and tangents — :doc:`Part II <../../part_02_fundamental_constructions/index>`
* **Applications XXVII–XLVIII** (pp. 15–36): Incircle, capable segment, common tangents, multiples/division, fourth/third/mean proportionals, Golden Section, inversion, polarity, radical axes, similitude, harmonic conjugates, Catalan's square, and Chasles's ellipse axes — :doc:`Part III <../../part_03_circles_and_tangency/index>`

.. folio:: 0082
   :page: p. 66

* **Principes** (pp. 36–39): Five guiding laws and simultaneous :math:`1/10, 1/15` equilateral division (**FIG. 10**)
* **Applications XLIX–LXIII** (pp. 40–52): Normal coordinates (**FIG. 11**) and modern triangle centers (:math:`X(2), X(6)`, Brocard, Steiner, Tarry, Gergonne, Spieker, Nagel, Neuberg, :math:`W`)
* **Observations diverses** (pp. 52–60) & **Note complémentaire** (pp. 60–64): Methodological foundations and replies to Mannheim, Brocard, d'Ocagne, and Laisant — :doc:`Part IV <../index>`
