2. Steiner’s Eight Fundamental Problems (Drittes Kapitel, § 18, [68]–[85])¶

  1. Erste Aufgabe ([68]–[71]): Parallel through external point to a diameter (\(11R_1 + 6R_2, S = 17\)) or general line.

  2. Zweite Aufgabe ([71]): Harmonic bisection and rational multiplication/\(n\)-section of a segment.

  3. Dritte Aufgabe ([71]–[75]): Perpendicular to a line (orthocenter on fixed-circle diameter: \(10R_1 + 5R_2, S = 15\)).

  4. Vierte Aufgabe ([75]): Copying a given angle to a given vertex and ray via parallel diameters of \(M\).

  5. Fünfte Aufgabe ([75]–[76]): Bisecting a given angle via parallel diameters and subtended chord.

  6. Sechste Aufgabe ([76]–[78]): Transferring a given segment to a given point along a given ray via centers of similitude \(A, I\).

  7. Siebente Aufgabe ([78]–[82]): Intersecting a given line with an un-drawn circle \((M_1, r_1)\) via homothetic secant on fixed circle \(M\).

  8. Achte Aufgabe ([82]–[85]): Intersecting two un-drawn circles \((M_1, r_1)\) and \((M_2, r_2)\) via their radical axis (Linie der gleichen Potenzen) reduced to Problem 7.
