Problem XXVII: Incircle & Excircles of a Triangle — Scholarly Notes¶

Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Prob. XXVII, fols. 0031–0032)

  • Primary Witness: Folios 0031–0032 (printed pp. 15–16); FIG. 1 appears on folio 0031 (p. 15).

  • Scan-Verified Geometric Fidelity: Inspecting 0031.png directly clarifies that Lemoine’s actual \(S = 23\) incircle construction does not draw the angle bisectors \(BO, CO\) at all; instead it leverages \(AD = b, BE = a\) along ray \(BA\) so \(DE = 2(s - a)\), placing both contact points \(Q, R\) and the incenter \(O\) via two perpendicular bisectors \(OR, OQ\) sharing the single circle \(A(DE)\).

  • Cross-References: Euclid Elements IV.4 (G.116), Clark Kimberling’s ETC \(X(1)\) (kimberling-etc), Mascheroni Libro X § 149, and Steiner § 18 Third & Fifth Problems.
