Problem XXIX: Common Tangents to Two Circles — Scholarly Notes¶

Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Prob. XXIX, fols. 0032–0035)

  • Primary Witness: Folios 0032–0035 (printed pp. 16–19); FIG. 3 appears on folio 0034 (p. 18).

  • Steiner Connection: The centers of similitude (Aehnlichkeitspunkte, aehnlichkeitspunkte) constructed here in FIG. 3 form the exact projective engine of Jakob Steiner’s Zweites Kapitel (§§ 12–13) and Sixth–Eighth Problems (steiner-1833), where Steiner projects an un-drawn circle onto his fixed circle \(M\).

  • First Columnar Accounting Table: Folio 0033 (p. 17) introduces Lemoine’s signature five-column ledger (\(R_1 | R_2 | C_1 | C_2 | C_3\)) for auditing complex multi-step constructions.
