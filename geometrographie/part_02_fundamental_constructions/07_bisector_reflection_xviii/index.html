Problems XVIII & XVIII bis: Perpendicular Bisector & Point Reflection¶

Émile Lemoine — La Géométrographie (English Translation, p. 10, fols. 0026)

XVIII. — To trace the perpendicular at its midpoint to a straight line bounded by two points, or to place the midpoint of a given length.

Symbol: \(\mathrm{op.} : (2R_1 + R_2 + 2C_1 + 2C_3)\); simplicity 7; exactitude 4; 1 straight line, 2 circles.

XVIII bis. — To place the symmetric point A of a point A with respect to a given straight line BC.

From two arbitrary points B, C of BC, I describe the circles BBA, CCA which intersect at A:

\(\mathrm{Op.} : (2C_1 + 2C_2 + 2C_3)\); simplicity 6; exactitude 4; 2 circles.

One may also describe AR which cuts BC at B and at C; describe BAB, CAB which intersect at A:

\(\mathrm{Op.} : (3C_1 + 3C_3)\); simplicity 6; exactitude 3; 3 circles.

If the undrawn straight line BC were given by two points B and C, the symbol would be:

\[\mathrm{Op.} : (4C_1 + 2C_3).\]
