Problems XIX–XX: Circle Center & Perpendiculars — Summary¶

Executive Summary & Complexity Metrics (Probs. XIX–XX, pp. 10–12, fols. 0026–0028)

  • Scope: Problems XIX and XX (fols. 0026–0028, pp. 10–12).

  • Problem XIX (Circle Center): Three equal-radius circles from three circumference points: \(4R_1 + 2R_2 + 3C_2 + 3C_3\) (\(S = 12, E = 7\)).

  • Problem XX.1 (External Perpendicular, Euclid I.12): Compass-reflection shortcut cuts circle count to 2 (\(2R_1 + R_2 + 3C_1 + C_2 + 2C_3\), \(S = 9, E = 6\), XX.1 — Perpendicular to a Traced Line from an External Point).

  • Problem XX.2 (Boundary Perpendicular, Euclid I.11): Thales single-circle construction beats textbook method (\(4R_1 + 2R_2 + C_1 + C_3\), \(S = 8, E = 5\), 1 circle, XX.2 — Perpendicular Erected at a Given Point on a Traced Line).
