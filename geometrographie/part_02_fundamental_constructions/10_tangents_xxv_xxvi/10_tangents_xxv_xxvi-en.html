Problems XXV–XXVI: Tangents to a Given Circle¶

Émile Lemoine — La Géométrographie (English Translation, p. 14, fols. 0030)

Problem XXV (Euclid III.16 Por. — Tangent at a Given Point :math:`A` on a Circle :math:`O`). Classical execution joins radius \(OA\) (\(2R_1 + R_2\)) and erects the perpendicular at \(A\) by Problem XX 2° (\(4R_1 + 2R_2 + C_1 + C_3\)), totaling \(6R_1 + 3R_2 + C_1 + C_3\) (\(S = 11, E = 7\), 3 lines, 1 circle). Lemoine improves simplicity to \(S = 10, E = 6\) in only one straight line: sweep \(A(AO)\) (\(2C_1 + C_3\)) cutting circle \(O\) at \(B\); keeping radius \(AO\), sweep \(B(BO)\) (\(C_1 + C_3\)) cutting \(A(AO)\) at \(C\) (opposite to \(O\)), and sweep \(C(CO)\) (\(C_1 + C_3\))? Specifically, \(B(AO)\) cuts \(O\) at \(C\) and \(A(AO)\) at \(D\), giving \(AD\) tangent at \(A\) by equilateral-angle symmetry:

\[\mathrm{Op.} : \left(2R_1 + R_2 + 4C_1 + 3C_3\right), \qquad S = 10, \quad E = 6 \quad (1\text{ line}, 3\text{ circles}).\]

Problem XXVI (Euclid III.17 — Tangents from an External Point :g:`A` to a Given Circle :g:`O(R)`). Whereas classical Elements III.17 costs \(8R_1 + 4R_2 + 4C_1 + 3C_3\) (\(S = 19, E = 12\)), Lemoine gives two distinct \(S = 18\) syntheses on folio 0030 (p. 14):

  1. First Method (Concentric-Diameter / Perpendicular Bisector, :math:`S = 18, E = 12`): Draw line \(OA\) cutting circle \(O\) at \(C, D\), bisect OA at M, sweep MMO cutting OR at contact points T, T, and draw AT, AT: \(5R_1 + 3R_2 + 7C_1 + 3C_3\) (\(S = 18, E = 12\), 3 lines, 3 circles).

  2. Second Method (Secant Chord & Thales Circle, :math:`S = 18, E = 11`): Draw an arbitrary secant from A or bisect OA via equal-radius arcs so that circle MMO cuts OR directly at T and T along polar chord TT, yielding superior exactitude \(E = 11\):

\[\mathrm{Op.} : \left(5R_1 + 3R_2 + 6C_1 + 4C_3\right), \qquad S = 18, \quad E = 11 \quad (3\text{ lines}, 4\text{ circles}).\]

Note the striking instrument duality documented in XXVI — Tangents to a Given Circle from an External Point: when circle O is already traced, Steiner’s pure-straightedge polar construction (Problem XXXIX + two tangent joins) requires zero circles (\(16R_1 + 9R_2\)), whereas Lemoine’s metric synthesis achieves \(S = 18\).
