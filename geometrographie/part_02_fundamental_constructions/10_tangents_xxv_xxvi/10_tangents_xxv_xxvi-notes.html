Problems XXV–XXVI: Tangents to a Given Circle — Scholarly Notes¶

Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Probs. XXV–XXVI, fols. 0030)

  • Primary Witness: Folio 0030 (printed p. 14).

  • Scan Verification: Direct reading of 0030.png confirms the exact 1-line, 3-circle boundary tangent symbol \(2R_1 + R_2 + 4C_1 + 3C_3\) (\(S = 10, E = 6\)), which Lemoine subsequently invokes verbatim inside Problem XL (pole of a secant line, fol. 0046, p. 30) and Problem XLI (radical axis of tangent circles, fol. 0047, p. 31).

  • Steiner & Mascheroni Counterparts: Steiner (§ 10, [26]–[30]) draws both external tangents by pure straightedge via the polar of \(A\); Mascheroni (Libro XI, § 161) finds contact points \(T, T'\) by compass alone.
