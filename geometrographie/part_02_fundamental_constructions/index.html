Part II: Fundamental Euclidean Constructions¶

La Géométrographie (Pau 1892 / Paris 1893, pp. 7–14) — Problems XI–XXVI

Lemoine’s geometrographic re-evaluation of the canonical Euclidean textbook constructions: copying angles, triangles, Gaston Tarry’s S = 9 parallels, perpendicular bisector, compass-only reflection, Thales’s single-circle perpendicular, circumcircle, angle bisectors, and circle tangents.

Outline of Part II¶

Problems XI–XVI: Copying Angles & Constructing Triangles

XI. — Through a given point B on a straight line BC, to draw a second straight line making with the first an angle equal to a given angle DAE .
