Notice the exact precision of Applications V, VI, and VII on folio 0021 (p. 5):

  • Application V covers both tracing a circle centered at given \(O\) with arbitrary radius and tracing a circle of arbitrary center passing through a single determined point \(A\) (placing one compass point on \(A\), dropping the pivot freely on the blank sheet at cost \(0\), and sweeping \(C_3\)). This dual interpretation of \(C_1 + C_3\) powers Gaston Tarry’s \(S = 9\) parallel (Problem XVII) and the single-circle Thales perpendicular (Problem XX)!

  • Application VI (\(2C_1\), \(S = 2, E = 2\), 0 circles) isolates the act of capturing segment \(AB\) in the divider compass without yet tracing an arc; if one leg remains planted at \(A\) or \(B\) to sweep \(A(AB)\), the circle adds only \(C_3\) (\(2C_1 + C_3\)).

  • Application VII isolates laying off an already captured compass aperture along a traced line (\(C_2 + C_3\) from an indeterminate locus point, or \(C_1 + C_3\) from a determined point, \(S = 2, E = 1\), 1 arc).
