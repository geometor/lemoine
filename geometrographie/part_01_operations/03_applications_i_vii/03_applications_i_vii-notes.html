Elementary Applications I–VII: Straight Lines & Circles — Scholarly Notes¶

Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Apps. I–VII, fols. 0021)

  • Scan Verification Correction: Direct inspection of folio 0021 (p. 5) confirms that Application VI in the 1893 Pau memoir is specifically taking a given length \(AB\) (\(2C_1\)), and Application VII is laying off an already captured length (\(C_2 + C_3\) or \(C_1 + C_3\)), distinct from Application VIII (fol. 0022, p. 6) which combines both acts (\(2C_1 + C_2 + C_3\) or \(3C_1 + C_3\)).

  • Concordance Cross-Reference: Application III corresponds to Euclid I.Post.1 (G.1 in ) and III — Straight Line Joining Two Given Points.
