Various Observations: Idealizations, Oran Notation & Replies to Mannheim and Brocard — Summary¶

Executive Summary & Complexity Metrics (Observations, pp. 52–60, fols. 0068–0076)

  • Scope: Observations diverses (fols. 0068–0076, pp. 52–60).

  • Four Polemical & Methodological Pillars: 1. Ideal unbounded sheet & drafting boundaries (pp. 52–54). 2. Retirement of the 1888 Oran eight-symbol system in favor of \((R_1, R_2, C_1, C_2, C_3)\) and introduction of the équerre (pp. 55–56). 3. Reply to :person:`Amédée Mannheim <mannheim>`: Dissects Mannheim’s common-tangent radical axis (\(S = 58\)) vs. Problem XLI (\(S = 17\), XLI — Radical Axis of Two Non-Intersecting Traced Circles). 4. Reply to :person:`Henri Brocard <brocard>`: Dissects Brocard’s adjoint-circle construction (\(S = 74\)) vs. Problem LV (\(S = 38\), LIV–LV — First and Second Brocard Points omega and omega’ of a Triangle).
