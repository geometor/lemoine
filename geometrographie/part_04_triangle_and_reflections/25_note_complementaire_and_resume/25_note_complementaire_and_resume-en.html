Complementary Note & Analytical Summary (d’Ocagne, Laisant & Complete Index)¶

Émile Lemoine — La Géométrographie (English Translation, pp. 60–66, fols. 0076–0082)

In the concluding Note complémentaire (pp. 60–64), Lemoine engages in a cordial scientific exchange with Maurice d'Ocagne, Charles-Ange Laisant, Dunesme, and Choisy regarding physical drafting precision, weighted operation costs, and the future expansion of Géométrographie:

f. 0077 · p. 61

1. Discussion with :person:`Maurice d’Ocagne <docagne>` on Exactitude vs. Physical Error (pp. 60–62). d'Ocagne—creator of nomography—raised the natural engineer’s question: should elementary acts \(R_1, C_1, C_2\) receive unequal empirical weights reflecting physical error variances, and how does \(E\) interact with oblique intersection angles?

f. 0078 · p. 62

Lemoine replies with remarkable clarity: weighting operations differently would depend on the craftsman’s hand, paper grain, pencil sharpness, and instrument brand, destroying the mathematical universality of the symbol! By retaining unweighted integer coefficients \((l_1, l_2, m_1, m_2, m_3)\), the symbol remains an exact combinatorial invariant while preserving full detail so any practitioner can apply custom empirical weights to \(l_1, l_2, m_1, m_2, m_3\) whenever desired.

f. 0079 · p. 63

2. Correspondence with :person:`Charles-Ange Laisant <laisant>`, Dunesme & Choisy (pp. 63–64).

f. 0080 · p. 64

Lemoine acknowledges simplifications and critical suggestions communicated by Laisant (his future co-founder of L’Intermédiaire des Mathématiciens in 1894 and co-editor of the Scientia series), alongside engineers Dunesme and Choisy, closing the body of the Pau memoir with an invitation for geometers everywhere to benchmark further constructions—an invitation fulfilled in the 1902 Scientia No. 18 monograph (lemoine-1902) integrating Géométrographie de l'équerre, Géométrographie Mascheronienne, and Géométrographie Steinerienne.

f. 0081 · p. 65

Résumé analytique par ordre de matières (Analytical Index, pp. 65–66)¶

Pages 65–66 of the 1893 Gauthier-Villars print present Lemoine’s canonical Résumé analytique, indexing the entire treatise in exact structural order:

  • Introduction (pp. 1–2) & Exposition (pp. 3–5): Part I

  • Applications I–X (pp. 5–6): Elementary straightedge and compass primitives — Part I

  • Applications XI–XXVI (pp. 7–14): Angles, triangles, parallels, bisectors, reflection, perpendiculars, circumcircle, and tangents — Part II

  • Applications XXVII–XLVIII (pp. 15–36): Incircle, capable segment, common tangents, multiples/division, fourth/third/mean proportionals, Golden Section, inversion, polarity, radical axes, similitude, harmonic conjugates, Catalan’s square, and Chasles’s ellipse axes — Part III

f. 0082 · p. 66

  • Principes (pp. 36–39): Five guiding laws and simultaneous \(1/10, 1/15\) equilateral division (FIG. 10)

  • Applications XLIX–LXIII (pp. 40–52): Normal coordinates (FIG. 11) and modern triangle centers (\(X(2), X(6)\), Brocard, Steiner, Tarry, Gergonne, Spieker, Nagel, Neuberg, \(W\))

  • Observations diverses (pp. 52–60) & Note complémentaire (pp. 60–64): Methodological foundations and replies to Mannheim, Brocard, d’Ocagne, and Laisant — Part IV
