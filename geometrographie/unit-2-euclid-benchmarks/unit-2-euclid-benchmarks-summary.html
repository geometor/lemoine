2. Four Core Optimization Techniques Introduced in Unit II¶

  1. Unconstrained Initial Carrier (:math:`R_2` vs. :math:`2R_1 + R_2`): When constructing a figure congruent to given magnitudes (Problem XVI), start from a free line \(R_2\) rather than placing two points first.

  2. Aperture Persistence Across Operations: Retain an arbitrary sufficient radius across symmetric steps (Problems XVII, XVIII, XXII), eliminating redundant \(C_1\) measurements.

  3. Eliminating Un-Needed Auxiliary Lines (Reflection Lemma XVIII *bis*): Reflect points across lines by two intersecting circles alone (\(0\) lines, \(S = 6\)), turning reflection into a zero-straightedge macro reused throughout Unit III.

  4. Replacing Symmetric Compass Stepping by Thales Diameter Incidence (XX.2): Trade three circles and one line (\(S = 9\)) for one single circle and two lines (\(4R_1 + 2R_2 + C_1 + C_3, S = 8\)).
