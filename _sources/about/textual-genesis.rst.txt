Textual Stemma & Archival Provenance
====================================

Three-Stage Textual Genesis of *Géométrographie*
------------------------------------------------

1. **Oran Note (30 March 1888)**: *De la mesure de la simplicité dans les sciences mathématiques*, presented at the 17th Session of AFAS in Oran (Tome I, p. 161; separate offprint pp. 1–12), introducing an initial eight-symbol notation (:math:`\rho_1, \rho_2, \rho_3, \gamma_1, \gamma'_1, \gamma''_1, \gamma_2, \gamma'_2`) and the draftsman's set-square (*équerre*).
2. **Pau Memoir (September 1892 / Paris: Gauthier-Villars, 1893)**: *La Géométrographie ou l'Art des constructions géométriques* (pp. 1–66), replacing the eight preliminary symbols with the canonical five operations (:math:`R_1, R_2, C_1, C_2, C_3`) and establishing Problems I–LXIII together with Lemoine's replies to Mannheim, Brocard, d'Ocagne, and Laisant.
3. **Scientia Monograph No. 18 (Paris: C. Naud, 1902)**: *Géométrographie, ou Art des constructions géométriques* (88 pp., Série Physico-Mathématique n° 18), uniting standard ruler-and-compass *Géométrographie* with *Géométrographie de l'équerre*, *Géométrographie Mascheronienne*, and *Géométrographie Steinerienne*.

Archival Witnesses & Multimodal Verification
--------------------------------------------

Following the GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium Iron Law, all operation vectors, problem numbers, and citations in ``geometor/lemoine`` were visually verified page-by-page from 300 DPI archival page scans across three public-domain sources, with UTF-8 transcriptions and SHA-256 checksums recorded in ``resources/geometrographie/sources_manifest.json``:

* ``resources/geometrographie/sources/lemoine_geometrographie_fr.txt`` — Émile Lemoine, *La Géométrographie ou l'Art des constructions géométriques* (1892/1893 & *Scientia* No. 18, 1902).
* ``resources/geometrographie/sources/mascheroni_1797_compasso_it.txt`` — Lorenzo Mascheroni, *La Geometria del Compasso* (Pavia, 1797; Palermo critical edition ed. Gaetano Fazzari, 1901).
* ``resources/geometrographie/sources/steiner_1833_constructionen_de.txt`` — Jakob Steiner, *Die geometrischen Constructionen, ausgeführt mittelst der geraden Linie und Eines festen Kreises* (Berlin, 1833; Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60, ed. A. J. von Oettingen, Leipzig, 1895).
