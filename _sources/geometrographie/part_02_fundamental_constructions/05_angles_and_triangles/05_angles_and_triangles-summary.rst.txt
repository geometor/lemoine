Problems XI–XVI: Copying Angles & Constructing Triangles — Summary
==================================================================

.. rubric:: Executive Summary & Complexity Metrics (Probs. XI–XVI, pp. 7–9, fols. ``0023–0025``)

.. folio:: 0023
   :page: pp. 7–9

* **Scope**: Problems XI–XVI (fols. ``0023``–``0025``, pp. 7–9).
* **Benchmarks**:
  * **XI** (Copy angle, Euclid I.23): :math:`2R_1 + R_2 + 5C_1 + 3C_3` (:math:`S = 11, E = 7` vs. speculative :math:`S = 19`, :ref:`concordance-copy-angle`).
  * **XII** (Third angle :math:`\gamma`): :math:`S = 18` (eliminates intermediate ray :math:`OE` from the 1888 Oran paper).
  * **XIII–XV** (ASA, SAS, SSA triangles): :math:`S = 25, 21, 24`.
  * **XVI** (SSS triangle, Euclid I.22): :math:`4R_1 + 3R_2 + 8C_1 + C_2 + 3C_3` (:math:`S = 19` vs. textbook :math:`S = 22`, :ref:`concordance-triangle-sss`).
