Problem XVII: Parallel Through an External Point — Scholarly Notes
==================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Prob. XVII, fols. ``0025–0026``)

.. folio:: 0025
   :page: pp. 9–10

* **Primary Witness**: Folios ``0025``–``0026`` (printed pp. 9–10).
* **Printed Errata Corrected from Scan**: On folio ``0025`` (p. 9), the 1893 typesetter introduced three small typographical slips—printing :math:`3C_2` instead of :math:`3C_3` in the classical symbol, :math:`3C_3` instead of :math:`C_3` in the second line of Tarry's 1st method, and :math:`2R_2` instead of :math:`R_2` in Tarry's 2nd method—while all three printed invariants (:math:`S = 11, E = 7`; :math:`S = 9, E = 6`, 1 line, 2 circles; :math:`S = 9, E = 5`, 1 line, 3 circles) confirm the exact algebraic symbols restored here and in :ref:`concordance-parallel-external`.
* **Cross-Reference**: Compare :doc:`../../../about/mascheroni-steiner` and :ref:`concordance-steiner-parallel-circle-meet`.
