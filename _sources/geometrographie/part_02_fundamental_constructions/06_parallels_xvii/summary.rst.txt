.. folio:: 0025
   :page: pp. 9–10

* **Scope**: Problem XVII — Parallel through an external point (fols. ``0025``–``0026``, pp. 9–10).
* **Efficiency Trade-Off**: Both constructions by :person:`Gaston Tarry <tarry>` tie at :math:`S = 9` (down from classical :math:`S = 11`, :ref:`concordance-parallel-external`), illustrating the subtle duality between fewer circles (1st method: 2 circles, :math:`E = 6`) and superior alignment precision (2nd rhombus method: 3 circles, :math:`E = 5`).
* **Minimal-Instrument Bridge**: Un-traced variant matches :person:`Mascheroni <mascheroni>` Libro IV § 80 (:math:`5C_1 + 2C_3` for point :math:`D`), contrasting with :person:`Steiner <steiner>`'s First Problem (:math:`11R_1 + 6R_2` by pure straightedge via fixed circle :math:`M`).
