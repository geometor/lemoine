Problems XXV–XXVI: Tangents to a Given Circle — Scholarly Notes
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Probs. XXV–XXVI, fols. ``0030``)

.. folio:: 0030
   :page: p. 14

* **Primary Witness**: Folio ``0030`` (printed p. 14).
* **Scan Verification**: Direct reading of ``0030.png`` confirms the exact 1-line, 3-circle boundary tangent symbol :math:`2R_1 + R_2 + 4C_1 + 3C_3` (:math:`S = 10, E = 6`), which Lemoine subsequently invokes verbatim inside Problem XL (pole of a secant line, fol. ``0046``, p. 30) and Problem XLI (radical axis of tangent circles, fol. ``0047``, p. 31).
* **Steiner & Mascheroni Counterparts**: :person:`Steiner <steiner>` (§ 10, ``[26]``–``[30]``) draws both external tangents by pure straightedge via the polar of :math:`A`; :person:`Mascheroni <mascheroni>` (Libro XI, § 161) finds contact points :math:`T, T'` by compass alone.
