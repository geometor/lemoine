Problems XIX–XX: Circle Center & Perpendiculars — Summary
=========================================================

.. rubric:: Executive Summary & Complexity Metrics (Probs. XIX–XX, pp. 10–12, fols. ``0026–0028``)

.. folio:: 0026
   :page: pp. 10–12

* **Scope**: Problems XIX and XX (fols. ``0026``–``0028``, pp. 10–12).
* **Problem XIX (Circle Center)**: Three equal-radius circles from three circumference points: :math:`4R_1 + 2R_2 + 3C_2 + 3C_3` (:math:`S = 12, E = 7`).
* **Problem XX.1 (External Perpendicular, Euclid I.12)**: Compass-reflection shortcut cuts circle count to 2 (:math:`2R_1 + R_2 + 3C_1 + C_2 + 2C_3`, :math:`S = 9, E = 6`, :ref:`concordance-perpendicular-external`).
* **Problem XX.2 (Boundary Perpendicular, Euclid I.11)**: Thales single-circle construction beats textbook method (:math:`4R_1 + 2R_2 + C_1 + C_3`, :math:`S = 8, E = 5`, 1 circle, :ref:`concordance-perpendicular-on-line`).
