Problems XIX–XX: Circle Center & Perpendiculars — Scholarly Notes
=================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Probs. XIX–XX, fols. ``0026–0028``)

.. folio:: 0026
   :page: pp. 10–12

* **Primary Witness**: Folios ``0026``–``0028`` (printed pp. 10–12).
* **Pedagogical Insight**: Lemoine's *Remarque* on folio ``0027`` (p. 11) exemplifies how *Géométrographie* overturns textbook habits: the endpoint Thales trick (:math:`S = 8`, 1 circle) strictly dominates the standard centered-chord erection (:math:`S = 9`–:math:`10`, 3 circles) in *every* configuration.
* **Mascheroni & Steiner Counterparts**: Finding a circle's center by compass alone (Problem XIX) is **Napoleon's Problem** (:person:`Mascheroni <mascheroni>`, :cite:`mascheroni-1797`, Libro X, § 143, 6 arcs). In :person:`Steiner <steiner>`'s straightedge system, erecting perpendiculars uses orthogonal chords of the fixed circle (:cite:`steiner-1833`, Third Problem, ``[71]``–``[75]``).
