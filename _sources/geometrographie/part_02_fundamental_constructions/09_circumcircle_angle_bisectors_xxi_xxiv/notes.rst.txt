Problems XXI–XXIV: Circumcircle & Angle Bisectors — Scholarly Notes
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Probs. XXI–XXIV, fols. ``0028–0030``)

.. folio:: 0028
   :page: pp. 12–14

* **Primary Witness**: Folios ``0028``–``0030`` (printed pp. 12–14).
* **Self-Critique of 1888 Oran Memoir**: Footnote (*) on folio ``0029`` (p. 13) records Lemoine's candid confession that his 1888 Oran solution to Problem XXIV had been much more complicated graphically because he had not yet trained himself to hunt systematically for graphic economies.
