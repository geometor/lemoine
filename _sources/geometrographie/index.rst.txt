==============
Geometrography
==============

.. rubric:: *La Géométrographie, ou Art des constructions géométriques* — Émile Lemoine (1892/1893 & 1902)

.. folio:: 0015
   :page: Title Page (Paris 1893)

.. container:: title-page

   | **Author:** :person:`Émile Lemoine <lemoine>` (1840–1912)
   | **Treatise:** *La Géométrographie, ou Art des constructions géométriques*
   | **Primary Witness:** AFAS Congrès de Pau (1892) / Paris: Gauthier-Villars (1893, 66 pp., 11 engraved figures), collated with Collection *Scientia* No. 18 (1902)
   | **Format:** Synchronized 4-Track Book Codex (*English · French · Summary · Notes*) — 4 Parts, 25 Atomic Leaves (100 Track Pages), 100% Folio-Linked, 11 Paired Archival Plates

.. epigraph::

   “Geometrography aims to evaluate with precision the simplicity and exactitude of geometric constructions, and to replace habitual speculative constructions with genuinely geometrographic constructions.”

   — :person:`Émile Lemoine <lemoine>`, :cite:`lemoine-1892`, :cite:`lemoine-1902` (*Introduction*, p. 1)
