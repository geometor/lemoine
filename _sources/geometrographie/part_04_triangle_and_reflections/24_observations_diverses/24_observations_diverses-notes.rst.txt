Various Observations: Idealizations, Oran Notation & Replies to Mannheim and Brocard — Scholarly Notes
======================================================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Observations, fols. ``0068–0076``)

.. folio:: 0068
   :page: pp. 52–60

* **Primary Witness**: Folios ``0068``–``0076`` (printed pp. 52–60).
* **Textual Collation & 1893 Print Variants**:
  * *p. 54 footnote*: Lemoine poses the open probabilistic question of the ratio :math:`E_n / E_{n'}` of probable final errors between two constructions of simplicities :math:`n` and :math:`n'`.
  * *p. 55 footnote*: Notes exclusion of the :term:`équerre <equerre>` from high-precision constructions while referencing the 1888 Oran Congress (:cite:`lemoine-1888`, p. 9) for its symbolic treatment.
  * *p. 58*: The 1893 print reads ``5 droites, 3 cercles`` beside :math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (10R_1 + 5R_2 + 2C_3)` (Problem XLI uses 2 circles; ``3`` is a print misprint), and cites ``(construction LII)`` where Mannheim's Lemoine-point construction corresponds to Problem LI (:math:`K = X(6)`).
  * *p. 59*: Step 5° prints :math:`2R_1 + 2R_2 + 3C_1 + C_3` (typographical slip for :math:`2R_1 + R_2 + 3C_1 + C_3`, since the summed total on the same page is :math:`22R_1 + 11R_2 + 24C_1 + 17C_3`).
  * *p. 60*: Item 2° prints ``statistique graphique`` for *statique graphique* (graphical statics).
* **Historical Significance**: The debates with :person:`Mannheim <mannheim>` and :person:`Brocard <brocard>` converted *Géométrographie* from an abstract curiosity into an active international research program—immediately inspiring John Sturgeon Mackay's 1893 Edinburgh study of Euclid's *Elements* (:cite:`mackay-1893`), :person:`August Adler <adler>`'s restricted-instrument synthesis (:cite:`adler-1890`), and :person:`Gaetano Fazzari <fazzari>`'s 1901 Mascheroni edition (:cite:`fazzari-1901`).
