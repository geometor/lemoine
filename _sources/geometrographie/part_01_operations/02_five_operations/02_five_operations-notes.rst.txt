The Five Elementary Operations & Coefficients S and E — Scholarly Notes
=======================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Exposition, fols. ``0019–0021``)

.. folio:: 0019
   :page: pp. 3–5

* **Primary Witness**: Folios ``0019``–``0021`` (printed pp. 3–5 of the 1893 Gauthier-Villars Pau memoir).
* **Subspace Specializations**: Setting :math:`l_1 = l_2 = 0` yields :term:`Géométrographie Mascheronienne <geometrographie-mascheronienne>` (compass alone, :cite:`mascheroni-1797`); setting :math:`m_1 = m_2 = m_3 = 0` yields :term:`Géométrographie Steinerienne <geometrographie-steinerienne>` (straightedge alone with one fixed circle, :cite:`steiner-1833`).
* **Visual Verification**: Diagram ``/_static/figures/fig_elementary_operations.svg`` illustrates both the straightedge primitive :math:`2R_1 + R_2` and the compass transfer primitive :math:`2C_1 + C_2 + C_3`.
* **1888 vs. 1892 Notation**: See :doc:`../../part_04_triangle_and_reflections/24_observations_diverses/24_observations_diverses-en` (fol. ``0071``, p. 55) for Lemoine's detailed table explaining the retirement of the Oran eight-symbol system.
