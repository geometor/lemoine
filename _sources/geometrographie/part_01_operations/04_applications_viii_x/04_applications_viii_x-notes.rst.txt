Applications VIII–X: Segment Transfer & Equidistant Points — Scholarly Notes
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Apps. VIII–X, fols. ``0022``)

.. folio:: 0022
   :page: p. 6

* **Primary Witness**: Folio ``0022`` (printed p. 6 of the 1893 Pau memoir).
* **Scan Verification**: Direct reading of ``0022.png`` restores the exact canonical identities of Applications IX (*arbitrary circle through two points*, :math:`3C_1 + 3C_3`) and X (*equidistant point*, :math:`2C_1 + 2C_3`), together with Lemoine's :math:`n`-fold stepping formulas later invoked in Problems XXX and XXXI.
* **Minimal-Instrument Cross-References**: Application VIII corresponds directly to :person:`Lorenzo Mascheroni <mascheroni>`'s divider-compass transfer (:cite:`mascheroni-1797`, Libro I, §§ 10–11) and :person:`Jakob Steiner <steiner>`'s Sixth Problem (:cite:`steiner-1833`, § 18, ``[76]``–``[78]``), as well as :person:`David Hilbert <hilbert>`'s *Streckenübertrager* (:cite:`hilbert-1899`, § 36).
