Problems XLVII–XLVIII: Catalan's Square & Axes of an Ellipse — Scholarly Notes
==============================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Probs. XLVII–XLVIII, fols. ``0050–0052``)

.. folio:: 0050
   :page: pp. 34–36

* **Primary Witness**: Folios ``0050``–``0052`` (printed pp. 34–36); **FIG. 9** appears on folio ``0050`` (p. 34).
* **Exact Algebra of FIG. 9**: Setting :math:`A = (0, 0), B = (1, 0)` gives :math:`K = (\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})`, :math:`G = (\tfrac{3}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})`, :math:`I = (\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \tfrac{1}{2})`, and :math:`KI^2 = (\tfrac{\sqrt{3}-1}{2})^2 + (\tfrac{1-\sqrt{3}}{2})^2 = 2 - \sqrt{3}`, which equals both :math:`KC^2 = (1 - \tfrac{1}{2})^2 + (1 - \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})^2 = 2 - \sqrt{3}` and :math:`KD^2` identically!
* **Speculative vs. Constructive Contrast**: Problem XLVIII is Lemoine's signature case study proving that even masters like :person:`Michel Chasles <chasles>` routinely omit two-thirds of the physical drawing labor when a theorem yields *full* magnitudes instead of *positioned* semi-axes.
