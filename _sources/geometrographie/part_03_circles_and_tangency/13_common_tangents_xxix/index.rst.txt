Problem XXIX: Common Tangents to Two Circles
============================================

.. rubric:: Émile Lemoine — *La Géométrographie* (English Translation, pp. 16–19, fols. ``0032–0035``)

.. folio:: 0032
   :page: p. 16

**XXIX.** — *To construct the common tangents to two given circumferences* :g:`O` *and* :g:`O'`.

FIRST METHOD
------------

*First case.* — *The two circumferences are exterior* [*]_ ; there are four common tangents; let :g:`O` be the larger of the two circumferences.

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (24R_1 + 12R_2 + 13C_1 + 8C_3)` ; simplicity 57 ; exactitude 37 ; 12 lines, 8 circles.

If we have only to trace the two exterior common tangents or the two interior ones, we shall have only:

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (12R_1 + 6R_2 + 10C_1 + 6C_3)` ; simplicity 34 ; exactitude 22 ; 6 lines, 6 circles.

*Second case.* — *The circumferences intersect; there are only the two exterior tangents.*

We find:

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (14R_1 + 7R_2 + 10C_1 + 6C_3)` ; simplicity 37 ; exactitude 24 ; 7 lines, 6 circles.

*Third case.* — *The circumferences touch externally.*

We find:

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (16R_1 + 8R_2 + 12C_1 + 8C_3)` ; simplicity 44 ; exactitude 28 ; 8 lines, 8 circles.

Let us note that :g:`B''` must be placed at the same time as :g:`B'` because :g:`B'` and :g:`B''` will then serve to draw the perpendicular at :g:`A` to :g:`OA` which is one of the tangents.

*Fourth case.* — *The circumferences touch internally.*

We trace :g:`OO'` and draw at :g:`A` the perpendicular to :g:`OA`:

.. folio:: 0034
   :page: p. 18

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (6R_1 + 3R_2 + C_1 + C_2)` ; simplicity 11 ; exactitude 7 ; 3 lines, 1 circle.

SECOND METHOD
-------------

*First case.* — *The two circumferences are exterior to each other (fig. 3).*

I trace :g:`OO'` . . . :math:`\mathrm{op.} : (2R_1 + R_2)`.

To the notations of the first method, I add these:

I call :g:`A₁` and :g:`A'₁` the second points of intersection of :g:`OO'` with the two circumferences :g:`O` and :g:`O'`.

I take on circle :g:`O` the points :g:`α` and :g:`α₁` such that :math:`AO = A\alpha = A_1\alpha_1` . . . :math:`\mathrm{op.} : (2C_1 + C_3)`.

:g:`α` and :g:`α₁` are placed on opposite sides of :g:`OO'`; I take on circle :g:`O'` a point :g:`α'` on the same side of :g:`OO'` as :g:`α` and such that :math:`A'_1 O' = A'_1 \alpha'`; I trace :g:`αα'` which cuts :g:`OO'` at :g:`V` and :g:`α₁α'` which cuts :g:`OO'` at :g:`V₁` . . . :math:`\mathrm{op.}' : (4R_1 + 2R_2 + 2C_1 + C_3)`.

It now suffices for me to draw from :g:`V` and from :g:`V₁` the tangents either to :g:`O` or to :g:`O'` by means of one of the two solutions indicated by construction XXVI, and to remark that their symbols must be diminished by :math:`\mathrm{op.} : (R_1 + R_2)`,
since we can use in the first, as any diameter, the diameter :g:`OO'` already traced, and in the second likewise :g:`OO'` as the arbitrary secant that must be drawn; adopting the first construction, we have:

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (14R_1 + 7R_2 + 18C_1 + 8C_3)` ; simplicity 48 ; exactitude 32 ; 7 lines, 8 circles.

Adopting the second:

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (14R_1 + 7R_2 + 16C_1 + 10C_3)` ; simplicity 48 ; exactitude 30 ; 7 lines, 10 circles.

.. folio:: 0035
   :page: p. 19

If we have only to trace two common tangents, either exterior or interior, we shall have only:

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (8R_1 + 4R_2 + 11C_1 + 5C_3)` ; simplicity 28 ; exactitude 19 ; 4 lines, 5 circles.

*Second case.* — *The circumferences intersect.*

By employing the first construction of no. XXVI, we find:

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (8R_1 + 4R_2 + 11C_1 + 5C_3)` ; simplicity 28 ; exactitude 19 ; 4 lines, 5 circles.

By employing the second construction, we find:

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (8R_1 + 4R_2 + 10C_1 + 6C_3)` ; simplicity 28 ; exactitude 18 ; 4 lines, 6 circles.

*Third case.* — *The circumferences touch externally.*

By employing the first construction:

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (14R_1 + 7R_2 + 13C_1 + 7C_3)` ; simplicity 41 ; exactitude 27 ; 7 lines, 7 circles.

By employing the second construction:

:math:`\mathrm{Op.} : (14R_1 + 7R_2 + 12C_1 + 8C_3)` ; simplicity 41 ; exactitude 26 ; 7 lines, 8 circles.

*Fourth case.* — *The two circumferences touch internally*; as in the first method.
