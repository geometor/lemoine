Problems XXXVI–XXXVII: Golden Section & Inaccessible Intersections — Scholarly Notes
====================================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Folio Provenance, Textual Collation & Cross-References (Probs. XXXVI–XXXVII, fols. ``0043–0044``)

.. folio:: 0043
   :page: pp. 27–28

* **Primary Witness**: Folios ``0043``–``0044`` (printed pp. 27–28); **FIG. 8** appears on folio ``0044`` (p. 28).
* **Historic 1893 Typo Identified via Scan Inspection**: On folio ``0044`` (p. 28), the printed summary line for XXXVI(c) reads ``Op. : (2R₁ + R₂ + 6C₁ + 6C₃); simplicité 18; exactitude 11``—an unmistakable compositor's slip turning ``9C₁`` into ``6C₁`` (notice that :math:`2 + 1 + 9 + 6 = 18 = S` and :math:`2 + 9 = 11 = E`, confirming :math:`9C_1` identically!).
* **Algebraic Field**: **FIG. 8** is an exact construction in :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \sqrt{5})` with :math:`AM = AP = \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2}AB`, linking directly to :person:`Mascheroni <mascheroni>` Libro II §§ 38–42 and Libro IX § 128, and :person:`Euclid <euclid>` II.11 / VI.30.
